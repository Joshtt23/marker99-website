## Dining Room & Lower Deck Program
- **Experience**: Reservations-focused, full menu, curated pairings.
- **Menu Direction**: Latin–Mediterranean seafood, land entrées, tapas flights, house-made desserts.
- **Beverage**: Feature classics that complement dishes (e.g., Rioja sangria, citrus-forward martinis, rum old fashioneds).
- **Happy Hour** (Mon–Thu, 3–6pm):
  - \$5 sangrias & spritzes
  - \$8 chef’s feature cocktail
  - \$2 off tapas selection
- **Operations**:
  - Host controls staggered seating to protect kitchen.
  - Servers handle ≤4 tables; support staff clears/reset to keep pace.

## Tiki Lounge Concept
- **Space Setup**: Cocktail tables, lounge seating, stage corner for live music, atmospheric lighting.
- **Staffing Model**:
  - 2 Bartenders (one bar rail, one service well).
  - 1 Cocktail server for tables; add second when forecast >50 guests.
  - Barback on peak nights.
- **Menu**:
  - Rotating weekly theme (e.g., “Paella & Palomas,” “Havana Night,” “Mediterranean Mezze”).
  - Limited tapas: paella cups, charred octopus skewers, empanadas, seasonal ceviche.
  - Signature cocktail lineup tied to theme; batchable for speed.
- **Programming**:
  - Live music Thu–Sun evenings (Latin guitar, salsa trio, jazz fusion).
  - Themed nights with chef activation (paella pan on deck, tableside tiki punch).
  - Monthly ticketed events (rum tastings, mixology classes).

## Service Standards (Casual Fine Dining)
1. **Greeting (≤60s)**: Warm welcome, name check on reservation, offer lounge wait if table not ready.
2. **Table Marking**: Present menus, water within 2 minutes, napkin fold when guest steps away.
3. **Menu Story**: 30-second highlight of Latin-Med focus and current features (chef special, themed cocktails).
4. **Course Pacing**: Fire times communicated to expo; pre-bus before next course.
5. **Beverage Precision**: Cocktail delivery ≤7 minutes; wine service with pairing notes.
6. **Check-Back**: Within 2 bites/sips; resolve issues immediately.
7. **Dessert & After-Dinner Offer**: Espresso martini, port, dessert shareable.
8. **Farewell & Next Visit Prompt**: Mention upcoming theme night or lounge event.

## Revenue Impact (Conceptual)
- **Higher Average Check**: Pairing-focused dining menu + signature cocktails can lift check by \$8–\$12 per guest.
- **Increased Bar Throughput**: Dedicated lounge with themed menu shortens ticket times and boosts bar sales without overloading kitchen.
- **Improved Table Turn**: Fewer full-service seats (by 28–60) reduces bottlenecks; lounge absorbs walk-ins and waitlist.
- **Event Revenue**: Ticketed theme nights, private dockside happy hours, and certificate redemptions drive predictable mid-week covers.
- **Brand Awareness**: Consistent social content + paid reach + radio partnerships expand audience beyond existing locals.

## Metrics & Accountability
- **Weekly Dashboard**: Sessions, top channels, reservations/event inquiries, certificate redemptions, average check, lounge vs dining sales split.
- **Monthly Review**: Marketing partner presents insights + next actions; operations reviews staffing/guest feedback.
- **Guest Feedback Loop**: Comment cards or SMS surveys post-visit; share wins/losses in pre-shift.
