# Marker 99 Waterfront Relaunch Pitch

## The Headline
Reintroduce Marker 99 with two experiences that fit how guests actually want to dine:
- **Dining Room & Lower Deck**: Latin–Mediterranean coastal menu, curated pairings, perfect for reservations and celebrations.
- **Tiki Lounge**: Cocktail-first hangout with live music, weekly themes, and quick small plates that keep the vibe—and the bar revenue—high.

Launch in **January** with December dedicated to prep, training, and buzz-building.

## Why It Matters
- **Fix the bottleneck**: Fewer full-service seats and a dedicated lounge keep the kitchen from getting buried while letting walk-ins drink, listen to music, and stay.
- **Win back locals**: A refreshed, consistent story online and onsite rebuilds trust after the previous management issues.
- **Grow revenue**: Higher check averages from pairings, faster bar throughput thanks to a focused lounge menu, and repeat visits from themed programming.

## Core Moves (Simple & Executable)

### 1. Experience Split
- Dining Room/Lower Deck = full menu, “classics” cocktails, wine & rum pairings.
- Tiki Lounge = cocktail tables, live music stage, themed nights (e.g., Paella on the Deck, Havana Night).
- Staffing: 2 lounge bartenders + 1 cocktail server on peak nights; rest of team focused on dining service.

### 2. Menu Refresh (Sample Highlights)
- Dining:
  - Citrus mojo snapper with saffron risotto (pair with Albariño spritz).
  - Mojo skirt steak with chimichurri potatoes (pair with smoked rosemary old fashioned).
  - Mezze trio board for sharing.
- Tiki Lounge:
  - Paella cups & empanada flight during “Paella Night.”
  - Passion fruit caipirinha, hibiscus paloma, rotating sangria.
  - Dessert churro bites with dulce de leche dip.

### 3. Casual Fine Service Training
- Provide warm, unscripted service with a clear cadence: 60-second greeting, menu storytelling, 2-bite check back, curated dessert close.
- December: three-day workshop (menu knowledge, service choreography, beverage class) plus nightly pre-shift briefs.

### 4. Marketing That Fits the Budget
- **Foundations**: You handle website + GA4 + weekly dashboard. No extra cost.
- **Content cadence**: Shoot one half-day in December; reuse across January (3 posts + 3 stories a week). Staff can capture quick phone clips.
- **Paid support**: Start with \$300/month (Meta + Instagram radius targeting + retargeting). Scale only if returns are clear.
- **Radio tie-in**: Use the \$75/$50 certificates to drive lounge visits, collect emails via redemption form, and invite back with dining offers.

### 5. Launch Week (Sample Schedule)
- Tue: Paella on the Deck — chef cooks paella outside; small portions for lounge, full entrée inside.
- Wed: Wine & Mezze Pairing Flight — ticketed tasting with three small courses.
- Thu: Havana Night — live Latin band, rum cocktail trio.
- Fri/Sat: Dockside Sessions — headline performer, sunset happy hour specials.
- Sun: Brunch by the River — relaxed lounge seating, mimosa tower special.

## Cost Snapshot
- **Menu development & training**: internal labor (Chef + managers).
- **Content shoot**: one freelancer day (\$400–\$600) OR staff-led if budget is tighter.
- **Social management**: start with a freelancer at ~\$1K/month covering posting + light ad management (negotiate scope if they balk; bare minimum is consistency).
- **Paid ads**: \$300/month initial budget (can adjust based on redemption data).

## Expected Wins
- Reduce kitchen choke points, boosting guest satisfaction and reviews.
- Grow average check through curated pairings and themed cocktails.
- Fill slow nights via lounge programming and targeted promotions.
- Build a measurable marketing engine (dashboards, promo codes) without overwhelming the team.
