### SYRUP PREPARATION (Batch Weekly)

#### Simple Syrup
- **Ratio**: 1:1 granulated sugar to hot water
- **Yield**: 1 quart
- **Storage**: Refrigerated, 2 weeks
- **Usage**: Base for most sweetened cocktails

#### Agave Syrup
- **Ratio**: 1:1 agave nectar to warm water
- **Yield**: 1 quart
- **Storage**: Room temperature, 1 month
- **Usage**: Tequila cocktails, margaritas

#### Watermelon Simple Syrup
- **Ingredients**: 2 cups fresh watermelon (puréed and strained), 1 cup sugar
- **Method**: Heat watermelon juice, add sugar, stir until dissolved, cool
- **Yield**: 1 quart
- **Storage**: Refrigerated, 1 week (or freeze for longer)
- **Usage**: Watermelon Smash

#### Passion Fruit Simple Syrup
- **Ingredients**: 1 cup passion fruit purée, 1 cup simple syrup, 1 tbsp lime juice
- **Method**: Whisk until combined
- **Yield**: 1 quart
- **Storage**: Refrigerated, 2 weeks
- **Usage**: Passion Fruit Mojito, Palomas

#### Coconut Syrup
- **Ingredients**: 2 cups coconut cream, 1 cup simple syrup, 1 tsp vanilla extract
- **Method**: Whisk until smooth
- **Yield**: 1 quart
- **Storage**: Refrigerated, 2 weeks
- **Usage**: Markerita, tropical drinks

#### Pineapple Syrup
- **Ingredients**: 1 cup fresh pineapple juice, 1 cup sugar
- **Method**: Heat juice, add sugar, stir until dissolved, cool
- **Yield**: 1 quart
- **Storage**: Refrigerated, 2 weeks
- **Usage**: Pineapple Old Fashioned, pineapple variations

#### Ginger Syrup
- **Ingredients**: 1 cup fresh ginger (peeled, sliced), 2 cups water, 1.5 cups sugar
- **Method**: Simmer ginger 20 min, strain, add sugar while hot, stir until dissolved
- **Yield**: 1 quart
- **Storage**: Refrigerated, 2 weeks
- **Usage**: Mules, Dark & Stormy

### COCKTAIL RECIPES

#### SIGNATURE COCKTAILS

##### The Markerita
**Glass**: Rocks glass, salt rim (optional)
**Method**: Shake, strain over ice
- 2 oz Coconut tequila
- 1 oz fresh lime juice
- 0.75 oz agave syrup
- 0.5 oz triple sec
- 0.5 oz coconut cream
- 0.5 oz Filthy sour mix
- **Garnish**: Lime wheel
- **Prep Time**: 1 min

##### Venezuelan Old Fashioned
**Glass**: Rocks glass, large ice cube
**Method**: Stir, strain over ice
- 2 oz Aged rum (Appleton Estate or similar)
- 1 bar spoon (0.5 tsp) demerara sugar
- 2 dashes angostura bitters
- **Garnish**: Orange twist (expressed)
- **Prep Time**: 1.5 min
- **Note**: Muddle sugar and bitters with orange twist before adding rum and ice

##### Mediterranean Martini
**Glass**: Chilled coupe or martini glass
**Method**: Stir, strain
- 2.5 oz Gin
- 0.5 oz dry vermouth
- 0.25 oz olive brine
- **Garnish**: Lemon twist (expressed), 2-3 olives on pick
- **Prep Time**: 1 min

##### Spanish Gin & Tonic
**Glass**: Large wine glass or balloon glass
**Method**: Build in glass over large ice cube
- 2 oz Premium gin (Hendrick's, Tanqueray, or similar)
- 5-6 oz Mediterranean tonic water (Fever-Tree Mediterranean or similar)
- Fresh herbs (rosemary, thyme, or basil)
- Citrus wheel (lemon or orange)
- **Garnish**: Fresh herbs, citrus wheel
- **Prep Time**: 1 min
- **Note**: Pour gin over large ice cube, add herbs and citrus, top with tonic. Serve with long spoon.

##### Italian Negroni
**Glass**: Rocks glass, large ice cube
**Method**: Stir, strain over ice
- 1 oz Gin
- 1 oz Campari
- 1 oz Sweet vermouth
- **Garnish**: Orange twist (expressed)
- **Prep Time**: 1 min

##### Pisco Sour
**Glass**: Chilled coupe or rocks glass
**Method**: Dry shake (with egg white), then shake with ice, strain
- 2 oz Pisco
- 1 oz fresh lime juice
- 0.75 oz simple syrup
- 0.5 oz egg white (pasteurized)
- 2-3 dashes angostura bitters (on foam)
- **Garnish**: Angostura bitters design on foam
- **Prep Time**: 2 min

#### LATIN CLASSICS

##### Classic Margarita
**Glass**: Rocks glass, salt rim
**Method**: Shake, strain over ice
- 2 oz Reposado tequila
- 1 oz fresh lime juice
- 0.75 oz agave syrup
- 0.5 oz triple sec
- **Garnish**: Lime wheel
- **Prep Time**: 1 min

**Flavor Variations** (add to classic recipe):
- **Guava**: Add 1 oz guava purée, reduce agave to 0.5 oz
- **Mango**: Add 1 oz mango purée, reduce agave to 0.5 oz
- **Passion Fruit**: Add 0.75 oz passion fruit purée, reduce agave to 0.5 oz
- **Pineapple**: Add 1 oz pineapple juice, reduce agave to 0.5 oz

##### Paloma
**Glass**: Highball
**Method**: Build in glass, top with soda
- 2 oz Tequila blanco
- 1 oz fresh grapefruit juice
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice
- Top with club soda
- **Garnish**: Grapefruit wheel, lime wheel
- **Prep Time**: 1 min

**Flavor Variations** (add to classic recipe):
- **Passion Fruit**: Add 0.75 oz passion fruit purée
- **Mango**: Add 1 oz mango purée
- **Pineapple**: Add 1 oz pineapple juice

##### Caipirinha
**Glass**: Rocks glass
**Method**: Muddle lime and sugar, add spirit, shake, strain over ice
- 2 oz Cachaça (or white rum if cachaça unavailable)
- 1 whole lime (cut into 6-8 wedges, muddled)
- 2 bar spoons (1 tsp) granulated sugar
- **Garnish**: Lime wheel
- **Prep Time**: 2 min
- **Note**: Traditional Brazilian cocktail. If cachaça unavailable, can substitute white rum (called Caipirissima)

##### Mojito
**Glass**: Highball
**Method**: Muddle mint, build, top with soda
- 2 oz White rum
- 0.75 oz fresh lime juice
- 0.75 oz simple syrup
- 8-10 fresh mint leaves (muddled)
- Top with club soda
- **Garnish**: Mint sprig, lime wheel
- **Prep Time**: 2 min

**Flavor Variations** (add to classic recipe):
- **Coconut**: Add 1 oz coconut water + 0.5 oz coconut cream, reduce simple syrup to 0.5 oz
- **Mango**: Add 1 oz mango purée, reduce simple syrup to 0.5 oz
- **Passion Fruit**: Add 1 oz passion fruit purée, reduce simple syrup to 0.5 oz
- **Pineapple**: Add 1 oz pineapple juice, reduce simple syrup to 0.5 oz

##### Daiquiri
**Glass**: Chilled coupe or rocks glass
**Method**: Shake, strain
- 2 oz White rum
- 1 oz fresh lime juice
- 0.75 oz simple syrup
- **Garnish**: Lime wheel
- **Prep Time**: 1 min

**Flavor Variations** (add to classic recipe):
- **Mango**: Add 1 oz mango purée, reduce simple syrup to 0.5 oz
- **Passion Fruit**: Add 0.75 oz passion fruit purée, reduce simple syrup to 0.5 oz
- **Pineapple**: Add 1 oz pineapple juice, reduce simple syrup to 0.5 oz

## Training Notes

### Key Points for Staff
1. **Consistency**: Always use jiggers, never free-pour
2. **Freshness**: Check juice daily, never use juice older than 24 hours
3. **Syrup Rotation**: Label with date, use FIFO (first in, first out)
4. **Garnish Standards**: Always fresh, consistent presentation
5. **Speed**: Target 1-2 minutes per cocktail during peak
6. **Flavor Additions**: When customer orders "Classic Margarita with Mango", add mango purée to classic recipe

### Upselling Opportunities
- Premium spirits for classic cocktails (+$2-3)
- Flavor additions (+$1)
- Wine pairings with food
- Cocktail flights (3 mini cocktails, $18)
